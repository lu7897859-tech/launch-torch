## Core capabilities
1. **Supplier Verification** — 2-minute registry check (license name vs payment name, industrial zone address, production floor video) to tell real factories from trading companies.
2. **Customs & Landed Cost Audit** — volume weight vs actual, LCL destination fees, HS classification errors, duty retroactivity (90-day lookback).
3. **Payment Fraud Check** — wire fraud red flags: personal accounts, third-party beneficiaries, 100% upfront demands, soft L/C terms. Safe structure: 30% deposit / 70% after inspection.
4. **Sample Switch Audit** — high-grade-sample-to-production-goods switching; contract clause tying final payment to shipment inspection + third-party PSI.
